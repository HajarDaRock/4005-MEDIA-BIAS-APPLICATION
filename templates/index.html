Example of a Biased Article:

This article talks about Mark Carney’s moves around federal spending and cancelling the carbon tax. On the surface, it seems neutral, but the way it’s written—highlighting things like the "$4-billion hole" or saying the tax was cancelled “through administrative fiat,” adds a certain tone.
