What is Media Bias?

Bias often lives in framing: word choice, emphasis, and the details we highlight.

Biased Headline

Headlines can prime readers before the first paragraph. Dramatic wording or one-sided framing nudges perception toward a political leaning even when facts stay the same.

Biased Article

Article tone comes from what is emphasized: highlighting gaps, choosing charged verbs, or foregrounding certain quotes. Those choices can subtly tilt Left or Right while remaining factual.
