The Canadian Media Bias Checker is a student-built project designed to help users understand potential political bias in online news articles, especially within the Canadian context.

By inputting a news article URL or selecting from a list of known media outlets, users can quickly receive an AI-based assessment of the article's political leaning: Left, Right, or Neutral.

This tool was developed to promote transparency and media literacy, particularly for Canadians seeking to navigate a growing number of online news sources with varying editorial perspectives.

Key Features

  • Bias classification based on article content using a local AI model
  • Manual bias rating for selected Canadian media outlets
  • Error handling for restricted domains and invalid URLs

This project is academic in nature and not affiliated with any political organization or media company.
