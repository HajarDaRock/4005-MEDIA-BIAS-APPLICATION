Why we built this

We want readers to see how tone, framing, and word choice can tilt coverage Left, Right, or stay Neutral.

The Political Spectrum is a student-built project designed to help people spot potential political bias in online news articles. By inputting a URL, the app returns an AI assessment of leaning based on the article text.

Our analysis runs locally on your machine, so you can experiment without sending article data to third-party services. The AI model is balanced across Left, Right, and Neutral labels.

What Left / Right / Neutral means here
